Three steps and you have a video

Twenty to forty seconds of a bot trading, your voice over it, and a quick edit. That is the whole job — and step one starts on the real bot.

1

Record a bot trading

Open Smart Recovery Differ — the same bot from the trading dashboard, on simulation money. Set it up, start it, and record your screen while it runs. 20 to 40 seconds is plenty: enough to see a few trades land and the bot stop itself at the target.

  • On a phone: swipe down for the control centre and tap Screen Record. On iPhone, add it in Settings → Control Centre first.
  • On a computer: Windows — Win + Alt + R. Mac — Shift + Cmd + 5.
  • Record in portrait if you can. Sideways video gets less reach on every platform.
Open the bot
2

Add a voice over

A silent screen recording is not a video. Say what people are looking at, say Magic Bots Lab, and say the bots are 100% free. Your own voice or a realistic AI voice — both are allowed, and you never have to be on camera.

Openers that work:

  • You have probably never seen a trading bot actually place a trade. This one is running by itself — and it is 100% free.
  • Everyone says trading bots are a scam. Watch this one work, then decide. I use this, it costs nothing.
  • I used to sit and watch charts all day. Now I use this instead — free bots that trade for you.
  • This took me 90 seconds to set up. I use Magic Bots Lab and every bot on it is free.
  • If you have MetaTrader 5, you can do this today. Free Expert Advisors, any broker.
3

Edit and post it

Put the recording and the voice together in whatever you already use — CapCut is free and does all of this on a phone. VN and InShot work too.

  • Add captions. Most people watch with the sound off, and CapCut writes them for you.
  • Cut the first second. Start on the trade, not on you lining up the shot.
  • Export at 1080p and post the same video to all three of your accounts — that counts as one day.
  • Log it in Post log so the day is counted towards your month.
