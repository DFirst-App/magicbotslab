Before your first run

Three things that decide how this goes

Set both limits, every time

A take profit and a stop loss are what turn a bot from something that trades until the balance runs out into something that trades a plan. When take profit is hit the bot stops automatically. So does stop loss. Neither works if you leave it blank.

Recovery needs balance behind it

Several of these bots use martingale — they raise the stake after a loss so one win recovers the run. That works right up until the balance cannot fund the next step. A small starting stake against a comfortable balance is the whole difference. What martingale really costs →

Free does not mean risk-free

The bots are free and they do take profitable trades — that has been tested. But every strategy has losing trades, and nobody can give you one that does not. Why no-loss bots do not exist →
